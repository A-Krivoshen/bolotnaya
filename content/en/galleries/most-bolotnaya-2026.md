This is **not** the [2024 pedestrian bridge to Balchug](/en/galleries/most-balchug/) and not Luzhkov Bridge. The new walk is being built **in the bed of Vodootvodny Canal**: from the GES-2 House of Culture and Udarnik under Maly Kamenny to Repin Garden, so you do not have to step onto Serafimovicha.

The [city announcement of 2 March 2026](https://www.mos.ru/mayor/themes/14306050/) gives about **170 m** in length and **5.4 m** in width. The walk from the square to Udarnik and GES-2 should almost halve. Aim: **fourth quarter of 2026**. This is not a municipal report: phone stills from Yakimanka.

On 20 June the GES-2 ramp still looked onto a worksite under the bridge. On 7 September the white deck is already in the water — work toward Maly Kamenny is still going on.
