This is the Bolotnaya Square webcam: the project's only stream. The camera that stood on the square itself stays off.

Camera facing **Ulitsa Serafimovicha** near Bolotnaya Square. Besides the square and the canal, the frame includes the new [footbridge under Maly Kamenny](/en/galleries/most-bolotnaya-2026/): it is being built in the Vodootvodny Canal bed, from GES-2 toward Repin Garden. It is not the Balchug bridge.

## What you can see in the frame

The camera hangs on the facade of the [House on the Embankment](/en/galleries/dom-na-naberezhnoy/) and looks down, across the street, at Bolotnaya Square. The frame is wide, so most of the garden reads from left to right.

- **Repin Garden.** In the middle: the paths and the round fountain. In summer there are flower beds and lawns; in winter the fountain is closed and the trees are lit.
- **Vodootvodny Canal.** On the right runs the canal with its granite embankment. In summer the fountains on the water are on, and river boats and paddleboards pass by.
- **The 2026 footbridge.** A new footbridge is going up in the canal bed under Maly Kamenny Bridge, from GES-2 to the garden. You can watch the deck grow on the camera. Details and photos are in the [footbridge album](/en/galleries/most-bolotnaya-2026/) and in the note [“Grey facade and a footbridge in the canal”](/en/posts/most-bolotnaya-2026/).
- **The street by the garden.** At the bottom of the frame is the road: buses and cars, headlights and street lights at night.
- **The skyline.** Above the roofs of Zamoskvorechye stands the Kotelnicheskaya Embankment tower. At sunset the sky above it is usually at its best.

From 2 June to 8 October 2026 scaffolding mesh blocked the view. How that looked is in the chronicle [“Repair and scaffolding 2026”](/en/remont-lesa-2026/).

### Close by, but not necessarily in the frame

Bolotnaya is the low bank opposite the Kremlin. A few minutes' walk away are the Repin monument in the garden, [GES-2](/en/galleries/ges-2/), the Udarnik cinema, the Bolshoy and Maly Kamenny bridges and [St Nicholas on Bersenevka](/en/galleries/nikola-bersenevka/). The camera does not show them, or only at the edge, but they are on the [district map](#dmap-cam-serafimovich) above and in the [photo galleries](/en/galleries/).

## When to watch

- **Sunset and dusk.** The sky over Zamoskvorechye and the Kotelnicheskaya tower changes colour, then the lights come on along the paths and the canal embankment.
- **Early morning.** The garden is nearly empty and the street is quiet, so the square itself is easy to see.
- **Summer.** Fountains on the canal, a green garden, river boats and paddleboards.
- **Winter and New Year.** Snow on the paths, lit trees and New Year installations in the garden.
- **City holidays and events.** On City Day and other holidays the district gets busy. This is the easiest way to check what is happening on the square right now.
- **The footbridge build.** Every week or two you can see the deck in the canal change.

Stream time is Moscow time. Video is 1440p at 20 frames per second. If the stream does not load, reload the page: the stream status is shown above the player.

## How to get there

Bolotnaya Square lies between the Moskva River and the Vodootvodny Canal, in the Yakimanka district. The nearest metro stations are Tretyakovskaya and Polyanka, a 10–15 minute walk. From the Kremlin side, walk from Borovitskaya or Biblioteka Imeni Lenina across the Bolshoy Kamenny Bridge. The garden is open to walk in, entry is free.
