On 8 October 2026 part of the scaffold came off the House on the Embankment, and the project's only camera shows Bolotnaya Square again.

Watch it on the [Ulitsa Serafimovicha](/en/cameras/serafimovich/) page. The frame holds the square, the Vodootvodny Canal and the footbridge under Maly Kamenny. The camera that stood on the square itself stays off.

The repair, and why the stream was down from 2 June, is in the [scaffolding chronicle](/en/remont-lesa-2026/) and the [2 June note](/en/posts/cameras-maintenance-2026-06-02/). This is a Yakimanka resident's site, not the city website.
