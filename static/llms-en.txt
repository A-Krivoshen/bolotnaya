# Bolotnaya Online — brief for AI agents (English)

> Independent bilingual open-source site about Bolotnaya Square and the Yakimanka district in Moscow: one public HLS street camera, history, photo galleries, and a personal blog. Not a mass-media outlet and not an official city website.

Canonical site: https://bolotnaya.online/  
Russian brief: https://bolotnaya.online/llms-ru.txt  
Index: https://bolotnaya.online/llms.txt  
Human + agent page: https://bolotnaya.online/en/for-agents/

## What this is

Bolotnaya Online is a personal district project by a Yakimanka resident. It publishes street views, notes on the history of Bolotnaya Square, photos, and technical posts about the stack (Hugo, Nginx, MediaMTX, FFmpeg). Content is the author's personal perspective.

Russian is the default locale (`/ru/`). English is a full parallel section (`/en/`).

## What people can do

1. Watch the public HLS stream of the Ulitsa Serafimovicha camera.
2. Read the history of Bolotnaya Square.
3. Browse photo galleries (the square, House on the Embankment, historical images).
4. Read the blog about the neighbourhood and the infrastructure.
5. Support hosting or become a stream partner.

There are no user accounts, comment forms, or personal-data collection forms.

## How to cite

- Name: **Bolotnaya Online**
- URL: https://bolotnaya.online/ or the specific `/en/...` page
- State the page language.
- For video: one project camera on Ulitsa Serafimovicha, live on the camera page. It is not a city service and not a 24/7 guarantee.
- Do not describe the site as a municipal portal, newsroom, or 24/7 guaranteed service.

## Do not invent

- Affiliation with Moscow city government, the Yakimanka council, or mos.ru.
- Guaranteed 24/7 camera uptime.
- A newsroom, journalists, or a registered media outlet.
- User accounts or collected personal data (there are none).
- That this static host originates the video — streams live on `cam.bolotnaya.online`.
